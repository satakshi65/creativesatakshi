StayX – Powering Connections, Empowering Communities

About Project Live Link -https://stayx.co.in/

StayX is an advanced platform designed to transform co-living and hostel management through the integration of prepaid smart metering technology. By streamlining operational processes and enhancing transparency, StayX empowers both residents and administrators with real-time insights and efficient resource management. The solution not only optimizes utility usage but also cultivates a connected, collaborative community environment. With a focus on reliability and user experience, StayX sets a new standard for modern accommodation management, delivering measurable value and operational excellence for all stakeholders.

Serving as UI Designer and Frontend Developer for StayX, I developed a modern, intuitive web platform specifically for the co-living and hostel sector. Leveraging Figma for design and implementing responsive, accessible layouts with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and JavaScript, I focused on delivering a seamless user experience that highlights StayX’s value proposition. Close collaboration with stakeholders ensured alignment with business objectives and user expectations, resulting in a visually engaging, high-performing solution that drives both engagement and operational efficiency.

  • Role : UI Designer & Frontend Developer
