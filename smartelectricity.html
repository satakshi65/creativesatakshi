Smart Energy Metering Platform

About Project

It is a Digital-based, cloud-native Smart Utility Platform designed for the efficient management of electricity.

Responsibilities & Contributions

In my role as a UI/UX Designer for the Utility platform, I was responsible for the full design lifecycle, from initial concept to final implementation. I began by conducting thorough user research and usability testing to understand the unique needs of energy administrators and field technicians. This insight was crucial for designing wireframes, user flows, and high-fidelity prototypes that simplified complex real-time data into easily digestible visualizations. My focus was on creating a user-centric experience that was not only intuitive and responsive but also aligned with industry best practices and accessibility standards. I worked closely with product managers, developers, and data analysts to ensure a smooth, cohesive integration of design into the final product.

  • Engineered responsive user interfaces for seamless integration and monitoring across diverse meter types, networks, and communication protocols.
  • Developed a robust and scalable frontend architecture to ensure platform stability and high performance, even with complex data visualizations.
  • Collaborated with the backend team to guarantee smooth data flow and API integration, providing critical real-time insights.
  • Optimized application performance for speed and efficiency, enhancing the user journey from setup to daily data analysis.
  • Ensured cross-browser compatibility and accessibility to deliver a consistent experience for all users.

My work on the UI/frontend helped transform technology into a powerful, user-friendly tool. This platform empowers energy providers to achieve greater flexibility and efficiency in their metering operations.

  • Client : All India Major Utilities & Real Estate Companies
  • Role : UI Designer & Frontend Developer
